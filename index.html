01

Research

What happens to a person when the model joins the work?

Two parallel studies with Professor Amreeta Chatterjee at the University at Albany. One reads what students write about AI on their own time. The other measures what happens inside a team that is using it together.

01

Student wellbeing discourse on Reddit

NLP · HCI · Summer 2026

I mined four student subreddits, classified the posts with a locally hosted Llama 3, then modeled the topics with BERTopic and bge-m3 embeddings. The context window holds 8,192 tokens, so a long post stays in one piece instead of getting chopped into fragments.

BERTopicBAAI/bge-m3Llama 3 · OllamaUMAPHDBSCANCUDA
02

AI-mediated student collaboration

Survey · Statistics · Summer 2026

Survey design and quantitative analysis on how the design of an AI tool reshapes who does what inside a team. Likert composites read against thematic coding, looking for cognitive offloading and for what happens when the group has a model to lean on.

Survey designThematic codingLikert compositesCognitive offloading
03

Workshop: BERT and BERTopic, from raw text to research-ready topics

Teaching · Summer 2026

Designed and ran a hands-on session for beginners: live Python through a full pipeline, why transformer embeddings beat bag-of-words for this, UMAP and HDBSCAN in practice, and privacy-compliant labeling with a locally hosted model. I wrote the curriculum from scratch.

Curriculum designBERTUMAPHDBSCANLocal LLMs
